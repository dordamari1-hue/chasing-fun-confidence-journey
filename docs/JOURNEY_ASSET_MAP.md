# Journey Asset Map

## Purpose
Map each prototype journey to the emotional job and the video/photo assets needed, so media can be selected or produced without guessing.

This map is for the current V1 prototype:
Intro -> Q1 -> Route Reveal -> Q2 -> Confidence Module -> Final CTA.

## Global Asset Rhythm

| Screen | Emotional Job | Best Asset Type | Asset Energy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Intro | Open curiosity and make it feel alive | Short vertical hero video or strong image | Warm, human, ocean, movement |
| Q1 | Let visitor self-identify | Minimal media or no media | Calm, focused |
| Route Reveal | Reward the selected motivation | Route-specific short clip/image | Distinct, emotional, immediate |
| Q2 | Surface the hidden hesitation | No media or soft texture | Clear, light |
| Confidence Module | Reduce fear with method and proof | Proof clip/image, testimonial, behind-the-scenes | Reassuring, specific |
| Final CTA | Make WhatsApp feel human and low-pressure | Dor/team/trust image or short founder clip | Warm, personal, grounded |

## Route 1: Social Confidence

### Trigger
- Wants to meet people.
- Afraid to arrive alone.
- Wants to know if they fit the group.

### Emotional Journey
Alone or unsure -> seen -> group is intentionally held -> safe to ask.

### Best Route Reveal Asset
Use video/photos that show:
- Arrival hugs or first group moments.
- People sitting together naturally.
- Shared meal.
- Group laughing after surf.
- Someone who looks like they arrived solo but is included.

Avoid:
- Party chaos.
- Closed friend-group feeling.
- Too many people shouting at camera.

### Confidence Module Asset
Best:
- Solo traveler testimonial.
- Short quote over a calm group image.
- Clip of first-day connection activity.

### Final CTA Support
CTA message should feel like:
"Can I come alone and still feel part of it?"

## Route 2: Surf Confidence

### Trigger
- Wants to learn/improve surf.
- Worried about level.
- Beginner anxiety.

### Emotional Journey
Not good enough -> normal to start here -> progress is supported -> safe to ask about level.

### Best Route Reveal Asset
Use video/photos that show:
- Beginner standing on a wave.
- Instructor/team member helping.
- Someone falling and laughing.
- Board carrying/waxing/prep.
- Calm water entry.

Avoid:
- Only advanced surfers.
- Huge waves.
- Competitive/performance energy.

### Confidence Module Asset
Best:
- Beginner before/after story.
- Instructor support moment.
- Clip showing small wins.

### Final CTA Support
CTA message should feel like:
"Is my surf level right for this?"

## Route 3: Freedom With Support

### Trigger
- Wants reset from routine.
- Wants tropical dream.
- Wants aliveness and freedom.

### Emotional Journey
Stuck in routine -> taste of another rhythm -> freedom is held -> safe to explore.

### Best Route Reveal Asset
Use video/photos that show:
- Ocean open space.
- Sunset or morning light.
- Walking/driving to surf.
- People moving through destination.
- Calm solo moment plus group joy.

Avoid:
- Generic beach postcard.
- Too much drone footage.
- Luxury resort vibe.

### Confidence Module Asset
Best:
- A day-in-the-life sequence.
- Short montage: surf, food, open time, evening.
- Image that shows structure without rigidity.

### Final CTA Support
CTA message should feel like:
"Is this the reset I need?"

## Route 4: Guided Confidence

### Trigger
- Wants someone to organize everything.
- Needs to trust the team.
- Wants to understand how the trip works.

### Emotional Journey
Could be chaotic -> there is a real method -> someone thought about everything -> safe to ask details.

### Best Route Reveal Asset
Use video/photos that show:
- Team/founder in action.
- Behind-the-scenes organization.
- Briefing or preparation.
- Group moving smoothly between activities.
- Staff supporting people in water or on land.

Avoid:
- Corporate process visuals.
- Spreadsheet/itinerary feeling.
- Overly polished authority pose.

### Confidence Module Asset
Best:
- Dor/team trust clip.
- Visual of trip layers.
- Calm behind-the-scenes proof.

### Final CTA Support
CTA message should feel like:
"How exactly does this work?"

## Route 5: Value And Fit

### Trigger
- Wants to know if it is worth it.
- Price/comparison behavior.
- Needs clarity before committing.

### Emotional Journey
Is this expensive? -> price is not the only lens -> value is risk reduction and fit -> safe to evaluate in a call.

### Best Route Reveal Asset
Use video/photos that show:
- High-quality experience moments.
- Team care.
- Group depth.
- Surf progression.
- Destination plus human proof.

Avoid:
- Discount/deal visuals.
- Luxury flex.
- Generic "best trip ever" montage.

### Confidence Module Asset
Best:
- Proof of quality.
- Short quote about how held the experience felt.
- Visual of multiple value layers.

### Final CTA Support
CTA message should feel like:
"Help me understand if this is worth it for me."

## Asset Selection Checklist
For every asset, ask:
- Which route does this support?
- Which fear does this reduce?
- Which belief shift does this create?
- Does it feel real, not stock?
- Does it make Chasing Fun feel more human?
- Does it create desire without confusion?

## Immediate Asset Priorities
1. One strong intro video or image.
2. One route reveal asset per route.
3. One solo traveler proof asset.
4. One beginner surf proof asset.
5. One Dor/team trust asset.
6. One value/quality proof asset.
